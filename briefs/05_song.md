# 05 — Song Dynasty Landscape · c. 1000, ink on silk · film 29–35 s   (lead style — painted first; built the ink toolkit)
**Homage:** Fan Kuan, *Travelers among Mountains and Streams*. A monumental peak with raindrop texture strokes and
scrub on its summit, a thread of waterfall in a dark gorge, a band of mist, wooded knolls with temple roofs, a stream,
boulders, and a mule train on the trail.
**The road** slips away into the middle distance: `path(u)` shrinks the pelican to 0.28× and lifts it onto a mountain
trail, so it is a speck under the peak. **Gag (required):** "so tiny … that it takes a camera push-in to find it" —
`camera()` pushes in 2.6× during bar 2, finds it overtaking the mule train, and pulls back out in bar 3.
**Technique:** everything is ink on white multiplied onto aged silk: contour, texture strokes (雨點皴, axe-cut) and trees
are p5.brush strokes batched by tone in three depth layers (far, middle, near); washes and mist are 2D; the trail band
is baked a second time at 2.6× and cross-faded in so the push-in stays sharp; a silk weave pattern is multiplied over
everything each frame. Colophon and collectors' seals top left and right.
**Pelican:** fine ink outline with a breath of pale body colour, same line whether full-size at the frames or tiny.
**Music here:** guqin, with harmonics as the camera finds it.
