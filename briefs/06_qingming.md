# 06 — Along the River During the Qingming Festival · c. 1100 · film 35–41 s  (ink toolkit)
**Looks like:** the Rainbow Bridge passage of Zhang Zeduan's handscroll: ink and **light colour** on silk — fine
ruled-line architecture (界畫 jiehua), pale washes of ochre, grey-green, faded red and indigo, hundreds of tiny lively
figures.
**Composition:** the great timber **arch bridge** spans the picture. The road comes in at y = 800 on the left bank,
climbs over the arch (crest about 150 units higher) and comes down to y = 800 on the right bank: use
`path(u)` → `dy` and `rot` following the arch (zero within 7 % of each edge). Under the bridge the river with a large
grain boat lowering its mast, boatmen with poles, eddies in fine line. On the bridge and banks: stalls under square
umbrellas, sedan chair, donkeys, porters with shoulder poles, railings packed with onlookers; willows with new leaf;
tiled-roof shops with signboards and the scaffolding gate of a wine shop on the right bank (ruled lines!).
**Technique:** ruled architecture with `inkFine` straight strokes; figures as tiny ink strokes with a dab of colour;
washes in Canvas 2D (`K.ink.wash`) multiplied in. Batch strokes by tone (`K.ink.batch`).
**Pelican:** fine-line ink with light colour (white bird, touch of ochre on beak, pale red on the bicycle), the same
scale of line as the figures — but full size, so it is by far the largest thing in the picture; nobody remarks on that.
**Gag (required): "passers-by turn their heads to watch."** Build the crowd as many small figures with a head/hat that
can face left or right (and for a few, a pointing arm). As the pelican passes each one, it turns — a wave of turning
heads rolling across the bridge with the rider; on the crest (land it on t = 3.0) the boatmen below all look up and one
loses his hat into the river. Afterwards they stay looking down the road after it.
**Title card:** a title slip in the upper corner (zh bold, en, date) with seals; shop signboards may carry small
period-style characters of your own.
**Music here:** bamboo flute with gongs and drums.
