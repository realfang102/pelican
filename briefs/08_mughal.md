# 08 — Mughal Miniature · c. 1600 · film 47–53 s
**Looks like:** an imperial album page of Akbar/Jahangir's atelier: opaque watercolour and gold on burnished paper.
**Page:** an **ornate gold border** — wide margin of indigo or salmon ground with gold floral arabesque and flecks,
then ruled lines (gold, blue, white), then the picture. Calligraphy cartouches top and bottom hold the title card
(zh / en / date; flank them with gold cloud-band ornament).
**Composition:** a **court garden** (charbagh) shown with the Mughal stacked perspective: tiled terrace seen from above,
water channel with fountain, a pavilion with carpet and bolsters seen frontally, cypress and flowering trees, a hill
horizon with pale sky and birds. The road (y = 800) is a red-sandstone garden walk across the foreground.
**"Guests watch from several viewpoints at once"** (required): courtiers, musicians and attendants placed all over — on
the carpet, at a jharokha window, on a balcony, behind a screen, a gardener up a tree, a falconer — each drawn in the
miniature's manner (profile or three-quarter faces, jewelled turbans, patterned jamas), each seen from a different
implied viewpoint, **all looking at the pelican**. Fine outline, jewel colours (lapis, vermilion, malachite, orpiment),
gold details, minute pattern on textiles and tiles.
**Pelican:** a meticulous natural-history bird study in the manner of the court animal painters — delicate feather
hatching, soft modelling — riding a gold-inlaid bicycle.
**Gag:** eyes and heads follow it; on the downbeat of bar 2 everyone's attention snaps to it (heads turn in unison, a
cup stops halfway to a mouth, the falcon lifts off the glove, the musicians' hands stop). In a corner a court painter
at a small easel is already painting it — and the tiny painting on his board shows a pelican on a bicycle.
**Before / after:** an ordinary garden party → everyone left staring down the walk.
**Music here:** sitar and tabla.
