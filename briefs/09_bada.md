# 09 — Bada Shanren, Freehand Ink · c. 1690 · film 53–59 s  (ink toolkit)
**Looks like:** a xieyi (寫意) album leaf by Bada Shanren (Zhu Da): **a few strokes and wide expanses of blank paper.**
Raw xuan paper (`K.ink.xuan`), wet ink that bleeds at the edges, dry-brush streaks, extreme economy.
**Composition:** more than two-thirds empty. One leaning rock or a lotus stem and leaf done in a handful of wet,
graded strokes off-centre; perhaps a single small fish low down. The road is **one dry-brush stroke** dragged across
the sheet at y = 800 (flying white). A terse inscription and two seals near an edge serve as the title card (zh in
bold brush-like setting, en, date — still legible).
**Pelican:** minimal strokes, redrawn every frame with fixed seeds (`K.ink.line`, `K.brushStroke` dry and wet, `K.ink.blot`):
the body one wet blot with a paler bled halo, the beak one long dry stroke and a swelling pouch stroke, neck a single
curved line, the bicycle two quick dry rings and three or four strokes. Do not outline everything — leave gaps.
**Gag (required): "a pelican giving Bada Shanren's signature eye roll."** Bada's birds and fish glare with a white eye
and the pupil shoved up against the rim. Give the pelican a large ring eye; on a beat in bar 2 (ease the `profile` so
it almost stops around t = 2.5–3.5) the pupil rolls slowly up and round to the top — `p.eye` toward `[0.3, -1]`, a tiny
tilt of the head — holds, then it rides on. If you include the fish, it rolls its eye too, half a beat later.
**Bleed:** paint the static rock/lotus with p5.brush (`ink`, `inkDry`, `inkWash`) and add 2D halos with `K.ink.bleed`.
**Before / after:** stillness. Almost nothing moves in this picture; that is the joke.
**Music here:** xiao (end-blown flute), sparse.
