# 11 — Ukiyo-e · 1830s · film 65–71 s
**Looks like:** a horizontal ōban colour woodblock print in the manner of Hiroshige's *Fifty-three Stations of the
Tōkaidō*. Flat colour areas with visible **wood grain and baren texture**, a firm black key-block line of varying
width, **bokashi** gradations (deep Prussian blue falling from the top of the sky, a rosy band at the horizon), slight
mis-registration, soft cream paper showing in the lights.
**Composition:** the Tōkaidō road (y = 800) runs along a coast: old pines with twisting trunks beside the road, a
thatched tea stall with a banner, the sea with white sails, a distant snow-capped cone under the bokashi sky.
Travellers on the road and at the stall: porters with shoulder poles, a pilgrim in a wide sedge hat, a packhorse and
groom, a palanquin — small, deftly characterised, in indigo and brown.
**Pelican:** key-block outline, flat colours (white, yellow beak with a bokashi blush, indigo scarf), a traveller's
sedge hat on its head; wheels drawn like cart wheels in the prints.
**Gag:** on the downbeat of bar 2 a **sudden shower** — long, fine, slightly diagonal black rain lines sweep across the
whole print in two crossing angles (an homage to Hiroshige's rain) — the travellers duck under hats and mats and run,
the stall-keeper pulls in the banner, and the pelican just rides on, hat tilted, beak forward. The rain thins and stops
during bar 3; a traveller looks out from under his mat after it.
**Title card (required look):** a vermilion rectangular cartouche in the upper right with the zh title vertical
(`K.vtext`), a smaller square series cartouche beside it carrying en/date, plus invented publisher and censor seals.
**Technique:** bake colour blocks in 2D with grain (`K.noiseLayer` stretched along the grain), key lines with p5.brush
(`ink`); rain as per-frame thin strokes.
**Music here:** shamisen.
