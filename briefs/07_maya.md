# 07 — Maya Codex · c. 1200 · film 41–47 s
**Looks like:** a page of a Postclassic screenfold book (Dresden Codex): fig-bark paper coated in fine white lime
stucco — hairline crazing, worn edges, a vertical fold shadow, flaked corners showing brown bark.
**Technique:** confident fine black-brown outline of even weight; flat fills of Maya blue, hematite red, yellow ochre,
black, some left white. Thick red frame lines divide the page into compartments; columns of glyph blocks (invented,
rounded-square cartouches with faces, hands, dots — plausible, not copied) run across the top and down the sides.
**Composition:** the road (y = 800) is the base line of the main compartment. Seated deities/scribes in profile on
either side (elaborate headdresses, ear flares, long noses, one holds a brush and conch inkpot, one an offering bowl).
Below the road: a band of sky/earth signs. Top: two rows of glyph blocks.
**Pelican:** codex convention: heavy outline, flat colour, crest drawn as a feathered headdress, scroll eye, beak with
a curling **speech scroll** when it "speaks"; wheels with a four-part k'in-like rosette instead of spokes.
**Gag (required): dot-and-bar numerals beside the pelican tally its pedal strokes.** Count pedal strokes since it
entered — one stroke per beat (2 per second), 0 … 12 — and show the count as Maya numerals (dot = 1, bar = 5) in a
framed cartouche that the nearest scribe is painting. Each new dot appears exactly on the beat; **on the 5th stroke
(t = 2.5) the five dots snap into a bar** with a small red flash — the bar-2 highlight — and again at 10 (t = 5.0).
The scribe's brush hand dabs on every beat; the other deity's eye follows the pelican.
**Before / after:** numeral cartouche empty (a shell-zero sign) → final count of 12 (two bars, two dots) left on the page.
**Title card:** a compartment framed in red with zh/en/date lettered in the codex's black line.
**Music here:** ocarina and wooden slit drum.
