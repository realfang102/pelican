# 10 — Javanese Wayang Kulit Shadow Puppetry · c. 1800 · film 59–65 s
**Looks like:** the audience's side of a wayang kulit screen (kelir): white cotton glowing amber from the oil lamp
(blencong) behind it — brightest in the centre, falling off to brown at the edges, slow living flicker — with the
puppets' shadows on it.
**Composition:** along the bottom the banana-trunk (the puppets are planted in it) and a patterned border band; the
road line y = 800 is the top of that band. At each side a rank of standing puppets: refined noble figures with long
noses, slender necks, elaborate coiffures and batik-patterned skirts, and a pair of squat clown-servants — **original
silhouettes, not copies of named characters**. A tall leaf-shaped tree-of-life figure (kayon) stands at one side.
**Puppets:** intricately **perforated cut-outs**: dense silhouettes pierced with rows of dots, chevrons and floral
tracery through which the lamp shows; slightly soft edges; a faint doubled edge from the lamp's flicker; thin **control
rods** (to the hands and the central spine) visibly running down to the bottom edge.
**Pelican (required):** "The pelican and bicycle are intricate cutout shadow puppets. The control rods are visible, and
the wheels turn in jerky increments." Build it from `Rig.parts`: silhouette + pierced tracery on body and wing (rows of
holes along feather lines), ornamental pierced wheels; rods from the wing-hand, the body and the bicycle down to the
bottom. Set `stutter: 10` and step the wheels and crank in visible increments (e.g. 30° steps). Slight rocking of the
whole puppet as if hand-held.
**Gag:** on the downbeat of bar 2 the tree-of-life figure is flourished and shivers (as it does to mark a new scene),
the standing puppets' arms jerk up on their rods to point, and the shadow of the puppeteer's hand briefly appears at
the bottom adjusting the pelican's rod.
**Title card:** on the cloth border in the manner of carved and gilded puppet ornament — zh / en / date, legible.
**Music here:** gamelan.
